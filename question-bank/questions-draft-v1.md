# Mocko Question Bank — Draft v1

Organized by Type → Subsection → (Behavioral / Technical, where applicable) → Difficulty.

**A note on sourcing:** the uploaded guide had a lot of unusable material mixed in — most of its Investment
Banking section (roughly 90% of the document) was a verbatim copy of a paid, copyrighted commercial guide
(Breaking Into Wall Street's "IB Interview Guide"), footer citations and all. None of that text — their
explanations, their example answers, their exact phrasing — is used here. Every question below is written
fresh, in original wording, aimed at the same underlying knowledge or scenario the source material was
testing (you can't own the *idea* of asking about a DCF or a teamwork story, just a specific way of asking
it). The "General: HireVue" and "General Behavioral" sections of the source doc looked clean/original to
begin with, but I rewrote those too for consistency and to cut the repeats between them.

This is a first pass, not exhaustive — the Finance/Technical sections in particular have room to grow deeper
(restructuring, FIG, industry verticals, etc. all got lighter coverage here than the source material
covered). Flag anything you want cut, reworded, retiered, or expanded, and we'll keep building on this file.

## General

### HireVue

Short, single-question, on-camera format — plain phrasing, no multi-part setups.

#### Behavioral

**Easy**
- Tell me about yourself.
- What are your long-term career goals?
- Why are you interested in this company?
- What would you say is your biggest strength?
- What's one thing you're working on improving about yourself?
- What do you like to do outside of school or work?

**Medium**
- Tell me about a time you had to work with someone whose style was very different from yours.
- Describe a moment when you didn't have enough information to complete a task. What did you do?
- Tell me about a time you had to stay calm under a tight deadline.
- Walk me through a time you disagreed with a decision your team made.
- Describe a situation where you had to learn something new very quickly.
- What's a piece of feedback that changed how you work?

**Hard**
- Tell me about the last time you let a teammate down. What would you do differently now?
- Describe a time a plan you were responsible for fell apart. How did you handle it in the moment?
- Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news to someone who wasn't expecting it.
- What's the most significant mistake you've made professionally, and what changed afterward?
- Tell me about a time your judgment was wrong and you found out the hard way.

#### Technical

**Easy**
- What tools or software do you rely on most day-to-day, and why?
- Walk me through a project you've built or contributed to from start to finish.
- How do you decide which programming language or tool fits a given task?

**Medium**
- Explain a technical concept from your field to someone with no background in it.
- Walk me through how you'd debug a system that suddenly stopped working.
- Describe how you approach learning a new tool or technology on your own.

**Hard**
- Tell me about the most difficult technical problem you've solved, and where you got stuck along the way.
- Describe a time your technical solution to a problem turned out to be wrong. How did you find out, and what did you do?
- Walk me through a project where the requirements changed significantly after you'd already started building.

### General Behavioral

**Easy**
- Why do you want to work here specifically?
- What's your greatest strength, and how have you used it?
- Where do you want to be in five years?
- Why are you looking to leave your current role?
- What motivates you to do good work?
- Tell me about a time you worked well as part of a team.
- What kind of work environment brings out your best?
- Do you have any questions for me?

**Medium**
- Describe a time you had to manage several competing priorities at once. How did you decide what came first?
- Tell me about a goal that took real effort to reach. What made it hard, and how did you get there?
- Describe a time you had to give a colleague feedback they didn't want to hear.
- Tell me about a time you had to adjust quickly to a major change at work or school.
- Walk me through a time you took the lead on something without being asked to.
- Describe a time a team you were part of didn't deliver what it was supposed to. What happened?
- Tell me about a customer, client, or classmate you had a hard time satisfying, and how you handled it.
- How do you keep yourself organized when you have a lot on your plate?

**Hard**
- What's the toughest piece of feedback you've ever received, and do you agree with it?
- Tell me about a time you completely changed your position on something important after being challenged.
- Describe a time you had to go along with a decision you personally disagreed with.
- If I called your last manager or professor right now, what would they say your biggest weakness is?
- Tell me about a time your work was criticized in front of other people.
- What's something you're genuinely insecure about in your professional abilities?
- Sell me on something ordinary — a stapler, a pencil, this chair — in under a minute.
- Tell me about a time you had to defend a position that most people around you disagreed with.

## Finance

### Investment Banking

#### Behavioral

**Easy**
- Walk me through your resume.
- Why investment banking, specifically, over other finance careers?
- Why our firm, and not one of our competitors?
- What are your biggest strengths and weaknesses for this role?
- What do you think a first-year analyst actually does day to day?

**Medium**
- Tell me about a group project where you had to hit a hard deadline with limited resources.
- Describe a time you disagreed with a teammate about the right way to approach a task. What happened?
- How committed are you to this path if the hours turn out to be as bad as people say?
- Which groups or industries within the firm are you most drawn to, and why?
- You don't have direct finance experience — what makes you confident you can handle the technical side of this job?
- Tell me about the most difficult decision you've made as part of a team, and what you'd change in hindsight.

**Hard**
- Walk me through a time you were the reason something at work or school didn't go as planned.
- If you already interned somewhere adjacent to this industry, why didn't you get a return offer — or why didn't you take one?
- You took time in a different field before applying here. Convince me this isn't just a backup plan.
- What's a real weakness of yours that would concern me if I hired you for this specific job?
- Tell me about a time you had to push back on someone more senior than you. How did it go?
- Give me a specific example of when you found and flagged a mistake nobody else on the team had caught.

#### Technical

**Easy**
- What are the three core financial statements, and what does each one tell you?
- What's the difference between Enterprise Value and Equity Value?
- In plain terms, what does the time value of money mean?
- What is EBITDA, and why do people use it instead of net income?
- Why would a company be worth more to a strategic acquirer than to a financial buyer?
- What's the difference between accounts receivable and accounts payable?

**Medium**
- Walk me through how the three financial statements connect to one another.
- A company buys a piece of equipment for cash. How does that flow through all three statements?
- What's the difference between unlevered and levered free cash flow, and when would you use each?
- Explain how you'd build out a basic DCF, step by step.
- What is WACC, and intuitively, why does it matter for valuation?
- Walk me through the three most common valuation methodologies, and one advantage and disadvantage of each.
- What happens to Goodwill on the balance sheet after an acquisition?
- Why might two companies with the same Enterprise Value have very different Equity Values?

**Hard**
- Walk me through how you'd calculate Terminal Value in a DCF, and defend which method you'd use and why.
- A deal is funded with 100% stock. Under what conditions would that deal be accretive versus dilutive to EPS?
- How would you set up a basic LBO model, and what are the two or three assumptions that move the returns the most?
- Explain how a Deferred Tax Liability gets created in an M&A deal, and why it matters for the model.
- Would you rather maximize IRR or the money-on-money multiple on a deal, and why might those two goals conflict?
- Walk me through how a change in working capital flows into a cash flow statement and why it matters for a DCF.
- How would you decide between using the Public Comps and the Precedent Transactions method if they gave you very different answers?

### Private Equity

#### Behavioral

**Easy**
- Why private equity instead of investment banking?
- Walk me through your resume with an eye toward what's led you to investing.
- What kind of businesses or industries genuinely interest you as an investor, and why?

**Medium**
- Tell me about a company — public or private — you'd want to invest in, and make the case for it.
- Describe a time you had to make a decision without complete information. How did you handle the risk?
- Walk me through a deal, internship project, or case study where you had to evaluate whether something was actually a good investment.
- How do you spend your time outside of work, and how does that inform how you think about businesses?

**Hard**
- Pitch me a stock or a private business as if you were pitching a fund's investment committee, in two minutes.
- Tell me about a time your initial read on an opportunity turned out to be wrong. What did you miss?
- If you had $50,000 of your own money to invest in one company right now, what would it be and why?
- Walk me through a disagreement you had with a manager or supervisor about how to evaluate a decision.

#### Technical

**Easy**
- What is a leveraged buyout, in your own words, and why does using debt make sense for the buyer?
- What makes a company a good candidate for an LBO?
- What's the difference between a strategic buyer and a financial sponsor in an acquisition?

**Medium**
- Walk me through the basic mechanics of an LBO model — what goes in, and what comes out.
- What levers can a private equity firm pull during its hold period to increase its returns?
- What's the difference between the entry multiple and the exit multiple, and why do funds care about the spread?
- Explain how a revolver is used in an LBO model and when a company would draw on it.
- What kinds of debt might a sponsor use to finance a buyout, and how do they differ in risk and cost?

**Hard**
- A fund buys a company at 8x EBITDA, holds it five years, and exits at 8x EBITDA with no multiple expansion. Where does the return come from?
- How would you set up the mandatory versus optional debt paydown in an LBO model?
- Walk me through how you'd decide between a dividend recap and an early exit for a portfolio company that's performing well.
- What's the difference between maximizing IRR and maximizing MoM, and how would a 3-year hold versus a 7-year hold change which one you optimize for?
- How does a add-on acquisition inside an existing portfolio company change the return profile of the original deal?

### Consulting

#### Behavioral

**Easy**
- Why consulting, specifically, over other career paths?
- Why this firm, and not one of its direct competitors?
- Walk me through your resume with a consulting lens — what story does it tell?

**Medium**
- Tell me about a time you had to change someone's mind about a decision they'd already made.
- Describe a time you had to lead a project without any formal authority over the people involved.
- Tell me about a time you had to resolve a disagreement within a team you were part of.
- Walk me through your greatest achievement, and why it matters to you.

**Hard**
- Tell me about the biggest professional obstacle you've faced, and what you'd do differently if you hit it again.
- Describe a time you had to deliver a recommendation you knew the client or stakeholder didn't want to hear.
- Tell me about a time a project you led went off the rails. What did you do, and what happened?
- Walk me through a time you had to influence a decision where you had no positional power over the outcome.

#### Technical

**Easy**
- How many gas stations do you think there are in Chicago?
- Estimate how many pizzas are sold in your hometown in a given month.
- How would you structure your approach before you even start estimating a market size?

**Medium**
- Estimate the annual market size for bottled water in the United States.
- A client's revenue is flat year-over-year despite steady unit sales growth. What are the first three things you'd check?
- Estimate how many dentists are currently practicing in a mid-sized country of your choosing.
- Walk me through how you'd size the market for electric bikes in a major U.S. city.

**Hard**
- A retail client's profits are declining even though revenue is growing. Walk me through how you'd diagnose the cause.
- Estimate the total addressable market for at-home fitness equipment in Western Europe, and state your key assumptions out loud.
- A client wants to know whether they should enter a new international market. What framework would you use, and what would you ask for first?
- Walk me through how you'd size the market for a subscription meal-kit service in a country with no publicly available industry data.
