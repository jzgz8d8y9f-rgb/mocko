Help

Common questions about how Mocko works.

What is Mocko?

A practice tool for job interviews. Record mock answers and get an AI-scored breakdown, or run quick skill drills like arithmetic, sequences, and risk-based decision making.

Is my recording stored?

Yes. Recordings are stored privately under your account so they can be transcribed and graded, and only you can access them.

How is my answer graded?

Your recording is transcribed, then scored against a rubric (content & structure, clarity, pace, and tone) using Claude, along with pace and filler-word stats pulled directly from the transcript.

Why do I need to log in?

So your recordings and scores are saved to your account and you can track progress over time, instead of losing everything when you close the tab.

Can I delete my data?

There's no self-serve delete yet, so reach out and we'll take care of it. Self-serve account deletion is on the roadmap.

What's coming next?

Full multi-question interviews, a live voice mock interview mode, more drills, and eventually friends and shared progress.
