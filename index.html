Getting Started
What is Mocko?

A practice tool for job interviews, in any field. Record mock answers and get an AI-scored breakdown, or run quick skill drills like arithmetic, sequences, and risk-based decision making.

What's coming next?

Full multi-question interviews, more drills, and eventually friends and shared progress.

Interviews & Scoring
How is my answer graded?

Your recording is transcribed, then scored against a rubric (content & structure, clarity, pace, and tone) using Claude, along with pace and filler-word stats pulled directly from the transcript.

Is my recording stored?

Yes. Recordings are stored privately under your account so they can be transcribed and graded, and only you can access them.

Drills
What do the drills actually test?

Timed arithmetic, risk-based decisions, and pattern sequences: the fast reflexes that show up cold in gamified assessments like Pymetrics, not the kind of thing you'd rehearse for a normal interview.

Account & Privacy
Why do I need to log in?

So your recordings and scores are saved to your account and you can track progress over time, instead of losing everything when you close the tab.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Delete Account. It permanently removes your profile, sessions, drill scores, resumes, and recordings right away, no waiting on a request.
